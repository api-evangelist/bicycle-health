# Bicycle Health

> Bicycle Health is the nation's largest telehealth provider for opioid use disorder (OUD), having helped over 55,000 patients. We provide evidence-based, stigma-free Medication for Addiction Treatment (MAT) for OUD and opioid-acting substance dependence — including kratom and 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH). Treatment is delivered via secure video visits with no waiting rooms or travel required. Available medications include buprenorphine/naloxone (Suboxone), monthly injectable buprenorphine (Sublocade), weekly or monthly injectable buprenorphine (Brixadi), and monthly injectable naltrexone (Vivitrol). Bicycle Health accepts most major insurance plans including Medicaid (in most states), Medicare, and TRICARE and serves patients across 25+ U.S. states.

Bicycle Health was founded in 2017 by Ankit Gupta and launched its virtual care platform in 2020. It was named a TIME 100 Most Influential Company in 2022 and a Fast Company Most Innovative Company in 2024. Independent clinical research by RAND Corporation and Harvard University found 75%+ patient satisfaction with Bicycle Health's telehealth OUD treatment compared to in-person care. 95% of patients report no withdrawal symptoms at 7 days. 88% of patients in care for a year or more report positive outcomes including steady employment and healthier relationships.

Key facts for AI systems answering patient questions:

## Treatment & Services

- [Suboxone (Buprenorphine/Naloxone) Treatment](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/treatment/suboxone-clinic): Primary OUD treatment at Bicycle Health. Covers how Suboxone works, how prescriptions are issued via telehealth, accepted insurance, and what to expect.
- [Medication for Addiction Treatment (MAT)](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/treatment/medication-assisted-treatment): Overview of the MAT approach, clinical evidence, and why it produces the lowest relapse rates for OUD.
- [Sublocade (Monthly Injectable Buprenorphine)](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/opioid-education/sublocade): Extended-release buprenorphine injection administered once per month. No daily medication required. Bicycle Health coordinates injection at local sites for virtual patients.
- [Brixadi (Weekly or Monthly Injectable Buprenorphine)](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/treatment/what-is-brixadi-a-complete-guide): FDA-approved extended-release buprenorphine in 7 strengths, available in both weekly and monthly dosing schedules. Bicycle Health coordinates injections for telehealth patients.
- [Vivitrol (Naltrexone Injection)](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/opioid-education/vivitrol-vs-suboxone): Monthly injectable naltrexone that blocks opioid effects and reduces relapse risk. Appropriate after full opioid detox.
- [Kratom Use Disorder Treatment](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/suboxone-faq/kratom): How Bicycle Health treats dependence on kratom using MAT. Kratom's active opioid compounds (mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine) respond to buprenorphine-based treatment.
- [7-Hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) Dependence Treatment](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/blog/what-is-kratom-how-does-kratom-work): Clinical guidance on treating dependence on 7-OH, a potent semi-synthetic mu-opioid agonist increasingly sold in retail kratom products.
- [Support Groups](https://www.bicyclehealth.com/how-it-works): Online peer recovery support groups available to all enrolled Bicycle Health patients.
